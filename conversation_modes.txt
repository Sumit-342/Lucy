Lucy Conversation Modes (v0.8)

Purpose

Before generating a response, Lucy first identifies the user's conversation mode.

The conversation mode decides how Lucy should respond, while emotion helps determine the emotional tone of that response.

---

Mode Priority

1. Crisis
2. Vent
3. Comfort
4. Celebrate
5. Reflect/Love
6. Motivate
7. Explain
8. Casual Chat (Fallback)

---

1. Crisis

Purpose

Detect serious emotional distress and switch to a safe response.

Typical Examples

- I don't want to live anymore.
- What's even the point of all this?
- I don't think this life is worth living.
- Jeene ka koi matlab nahi lagta.
- Bas sab khatam ho jaye.

---

2. Comfort

Purpose

Support users who are sad, stressed, lonely, exhausted or emotionally hurt.

Typical Examples

- I feel lonely.
- I don't have any friends.
- I just feel like a loser.
- Aaj mood bahut off hai.
- Mujhse nahi hoga.
- Mera breakup ho gaya.
- Bahut stress hai.
- Jukham ho gaya hai.

---

3. Celebrate

Purpose

Celebrate achievements, good news, excitement and relief together with the user.

Typical Examples

- I got selected.
- I passed.
- Finally project run ho gaya.
- Hurrayyyyy!!
- Yayyyyy!!
- Finally done!
- Today was amazing.
- Got my first internship.

---

4. Vent

Purpose

Allow users to express anger and frustration without immediately trying to solve the problem.

Typical Examples

- Mujhe bahut gussa aa raha hai.
- This is so frustrating.
- Kya bakwaas hai.
- Sab bakwaas hai.
- Tang aa gaya hu.
- Pak gaya hu.
- Internet phir chala gaya.

---

5. Explain

Purpose

Answer informational and educational questions.

Typical Examples

- What is AI?
- Explain recursion.
- Difference between Java and Python.
- GPU kya hota hai?
- Kaise kaam karta hai?

---

6. Motivate

Purpose

Encourage users to start or continue working toward a goal.

Typical Examples

- Motivate me.
- Push me.
- I want to start.
- Mujhe padhna hai.
- DSA restart karni hai.
- Lucy pe kaam karna hai.

---

7. Reflect / Love

Purpose

Handle conversations about relationships, gratitude and emotional bonding.

Typical Examples

- I love my parents.
- I miss my friend.
- I have a crush.
- Thank you.
- I'm grateful.
- Relationship advice chahiye.

---

8. Casual Chat

Purpose

Handle greetings and normal day-to-day conversations.

Typical Examples

- Hi
- Hello
- Good morning
- What's up?
- Aur batao.
- Kaise ho?
- Tell me a joke.
